## Two plausible supplier channels (official publisher documentation)

### Awin (potential primary UK channel)
- Awin's UK publisher FAQ says voucher-code platforms are eligible business models but the publisher application is reviewed by compliance. Source: https://www.awin.com/gb/faqs
- Publisher website and promotional methods must be genuine; after network approval, apply to **individual** advertiser programmes. Network approval **does not mean merchant approval**.
- FAQ says signup involves a reimbursable verification deposit; confirm current amount, business eligibility, VAT/tax requirements and applicable country rules before applying.
- Operational fit: start with a properly identified website publisher. Collect only advertiser-approved codes, accurate valid-from/expires, exclusions, destinations, commission and reversal terms.

### impact.com (alternative to compare)
- Publisher profile / marketplace application and website verification required before discovering and applying to specific brands. Source: https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/working-with-brands/applying-to-brands/join-the-brands-marketplace
- Brands and publisher terms differ by geography and program; an approved marketplace account does not automatically grant individual campaign rights. Source: https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/working-with-brands/applying-to-brands/find-and-apply-to-brands
- Do not assume UK eligibility, coupons, direct links, APIs or commission rates until confirmed in a merchant's own agreement.

### UK disclosure baseline
- ASA/CAP guidance confirms affiliate marketing on sites and apps is advertising activity where commercial relationships and affiliate content need appropriate, identifiable disclosure. Source: https://www.asa.org.uk/news/get-yourself-affiliated-with-the-rules-on-affiliate-marketing.html
- This summary is product guidance, not legal advice. Review the precise page layout, cookie/fingerprint practices, UK GDPR/PECR, withdrawal/refund terms, and CAP/ASA requirements before acquisition.

## Merchant evidence ledger — required per offer
Maintain a private admin or internal records table/document for:
- Verified merchant legal/store name, official store URL, participating affiliate network and programme ID
- Programme approval status, permitted publisher property, voucher-code permission, attribution rules
- Offer type (public coupon, exclusive code, automatically applied sale, affiliate-only), exact terms and exclusions
- Code/destination verified against official merchant feed or authorized partner terms
- Last checked time, tested checkout/cart conditions, expiration and subsequent recheck due date
- Real affiliate redirect / tracking URL and network proof of authorization
- Approval operator, evidence link and reason, last redeemed test outcome
- Payout basis, commission reversal window, paid/pending status, no assumed profit
- Suspension / complaint / broken-code response

**Hard gate:** An offer may enter `promo_codes` only after the source and merchant programme allow publication; `approved=true` by itself is not a claimed empirical success rate.
